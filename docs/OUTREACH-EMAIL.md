## Sending tips

1. **One personalised line per email** — quote a specific article, post, or boat name. Generic outreach goes to spam.
2. **Subject line is everything** — keep it under 50 characters, no marketing-y words ("introducing", "amazing", "revolutionary").
3. **Don't paste links until paragraph 3+** — Gmail penalises links in the first sentence.
4. **Send Tue–Thu, 09:00–11:00 local time** — best open rates for B2B.
5. **One follow-up after 7 days, never more** — short, polite, no guilt-trip. "Just bumping this up in case it got buried."
6. **Track replies** — when someone shares, send them a thank-you note 48 hours later. Builds the relationship.
