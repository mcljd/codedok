# Sea-Flux Teardown

Source: sea-flux.com / sea-flux.com/uk, Capterra, SoftwareWorld. Pulled May 2026.

---

## Their positioning in one line

> "Integrated fleet management software for commercial vessels."

Generic. Aimed at *commercial* and *fleet* — not at coded sport/pleasure or small charter operators specifically. That's the gap we walk through.

## What they sell (10 modules)

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| Planned Maintenance | Hourly + time-based tasks, link manuals/invoices, spare parts |
| Digital Logbook | Paperless, customisable, automation for crew hours and fuel |
| Safety Management System | Routine inspections, expiring-equipment alerts |
| Forms & Checklists | Builder, templates, customisable |
| Documentation Management | Cloud store for SMS, certs, SOPs, surveys + expiry alerts |
| Reporting | Custom reports across vessel/crew |
| Health & Safety | Risk register, incident reporting, H&S meetings, dangerous goods |
| Crew Management | Training matrix, cert tracking, role-based permissions |
| Hours of Rest | Track work/rest hours per individual or group |
| Real-time Vessel Monitoring | Equipment history, supplier tracking |

## The buyer signal (from reviews)

- One reviewer migrated **18 vessels off spreadsheets** — "real game changer".
- Praised: colour-coded urgency icons, all-in-one view, easy-to-use across non-tech-savvy crew, customer support backup.
- Complaint: **couldn't log individual personnel sea hours** (being remedied).
- 75% of customers report having dropped paper entirely.

The buyer is a **spreadsheet-graduate** running 5–20 vessels. Owner-operators with 1–3 boats are likely underserved.

## Sales motion

- **No public pricing.** Demo-gated, "fill the form, we'll call you".
- Free demo, free trial period unspecified.
- Apple App Store + Google Play apps exist but mobile UX is a side feature, not the centre of gravity.
- SMS module is **bundled** into the suite, not an add-on. Heavy lifting partnered out to Blythe Industries.

## UX/marketing weakness signals

- Generic "All-devices-v2-mockup" stock-feeling screenshots.
- "Foster a culture of safety awareness" / "empower action" — marketing fluff.
- No screenshots of actual workflows (cert renewal, scan, inspection prep).
- Heavy outcome claims ("75% paperless") without showing the *experience* that delivers them.
- Long sub-nav (10 Solutions items) — they're afraid to pick a primary use case.

## Where Coded Ok wins (the angles)

### 1. Niche-first positioning
Sea-Flux is "commercial fleets". Coded Ok is **"the app for UK & Irish coded vessel operators"** — Workboat Code Ed 3, Sport/Pleasure Code 2025, MCA categories, YDSA/RYA cert workflows. That niche language makes a £30k charter skipper feel seen; Sea-Flux makes them feel like a small fish.

### 2. Mobile-first, scan-first
Sea-Flux: forms → fields → save. Coded Ok: **point camera at the cert, OCR fills the form, confirm**. You already have the scan screen — make it the front door, not a side door.

### 3. One-tap inspection pack
Sea-Flux's docs are "stored". Coded Ok generates a **single signed PDF** the surveyor can read on their iPad — vessel particulars, all certs, crew tickets, equipment service history, last-inspection deficiencies. This is the headline demo.

### 4. Public, transparent pricing
Sea-Flux hides theirs. Publish ours on the marketing page. For a small-vessel buyer, "fill in this form" loses to "£19/month, start now".

### 5. Native polish
Their reviews praise functionality, not design. Apple-Wallet crew tickets, push (not email) reminders, biometric unlock, native scan, smooth motion design — these are the table stakes for App Store top-rated, and they don't have them.

### 6. SMS as a *productised service*
Their SMS is bundled and white-labelled to Blythe. Yours is a **clear premium add-on**: pick a template → answer guided questions → Joseph reviews & signs off → in-app version-controlled SMS. Sells as "we build your SMS" not "we give you a tool to build it yourself".

### 7. Skipper hand-off (a feature they don't have)
Generate a **time-limited QR code** that gives a relief skipper or yard inspector read-only access to one vessel. No login, no seat fee, no "ask Joseph for credentials".

### 8. Offline-first, sync later
They claim offline; we make it the *default*. Harbour wifi is bad. The app works on the boat, syncs at the dock.
