## Value proposition section

**Section header:** Built around MCA coding. Not around compliance theatre.

### Inspection pack in one tap
When your surveyor asks for the file, you hand them a file. Certs, schedules, crew records, safety equipment log — generated as a single PDF pack at the state the MCA expects. No hunting in WhatsApp threads or the filing cabinet.

### Never miss a renewal
Annual survey. ENG1. Pyrotechnics. VHF licence. Liferaft service. MMSI. Radio survey. CodedOK knows what's coming due on each vessel and each crew member, and tells you in the order that matters — overdue first, then "within 30 days," then "the one that'll catch you out in 60."

### Fleet-wide, or single vessel
One compliance score per vessel. One rolled-up score for the fleet. If you run two boats in Cushendall and one in Bangor, you see them side by side — not as separate spreadsheets.

### Category-aware
Cat 2, Cat 3, Cat 4, Cat 5, Cat 6. Motor charter, angling, dive, workboat. CodedOK knows the requirements differ and only shows you what applies to the vessel you're looking at. No scrolling past certs you'll never need.

## How it works

**1. Add your vessel**
Enter the SSR number. CodedOK pulls category, class, and the base cert requirements for how you're coded.

**2. Upload what you have**
Photograph certs with your phone. CodedOK reads the expiry date and slots each one into the right tray.

**3. Get through the season**
Green means go. Amber means book it. Red means fix it before you sail. When the MCA surveyor shows up, tap "Inspection pack."

## Brand voice applied

**Precise** — industry terms used correctly (coded, Cat 2, ENG1, SSR, pyro, survey). **Operational** — reads like a first mate, not a compliance officer. **Calmly urgent** — flags stakes (detained vessel, lost season) without catastrophising. **Pro-skipper** — positions the MCA as the thing you pass, not the villain.

**Avoided:** "holistic regulatory solution," "best-in-class," "AI-powered," "imagine if…," any patronising tone.
