How it works

Three steps. Roughly an afternoon.

Add your vessel, photograph your certs, and you're running a compliant fleet from your phone.

1

Add your vessel

Enter the SSR number. CodedOK pulls category, class and the base cert requirements for how you're coded — so you're not starting from a blank sheet.

2

Upload what you have

Photograph certs with your phone. CodedOK reads the expiry date and slots each one into the right tray. Keep going until your compliance score stops moving.

3

Get through the season

Green means go. Amber means book it. Red means fix before you sail. When the surveyor shows up, tap "Inspection pack" and hand over the phone.

FAQ

Questions skippers actually ask.

Which MCA categories does CodedOK support?
Cat 2, Cat 3, Cat 4, Cat 5 and Cat 6. Motor charter, angling, dive, workboat. If you're coded under the Workboat Code or the Small Commercial Vessel Code, you're supported.
Is this a replacement for my Authorised Person's audit?
No — and nothing will be. CodedOK keeps your paperwork audit-ready so that when your Certifying Authority comes round, the conversation is about the boat, not about where your ENG1 went.
Does it work offline at sea?
Yes. Once your data is synced, you can view certs, crew records and the inspection pack without signal. Changes sync when you're back in range.
I only have one vessel — is this overkill?
No. The single-vessel plan is priced for owner-operators, and the time saved on tracking renewals usually pays for the year in the first month.
Can my deckhand or mate access the app?
Yes — crew members get a read-only view of their own certs (ENG1, STCW, licences) and can upload updates for you to approve.
What happens to my data if I stop paying?
You can export everything as a PDF archive at any time. If your subscription lapses, your data is held for 90 days before deletion so you can reactivate without losing anything.
What does the surveyor actually see?
A single ordered PDF pack containing the vessel declaration, current certs, crew records, safety equipment log and survey schedule. The same documents you'd have in a binder — just in the order the MCA expects.
