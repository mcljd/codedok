CodedOK tracks compliance separately for each jurisdiction. UK and Ireland have different legal requirements — selecting both keeps you covered for cross-border operation.

🇬🇧 UK — MCA Sport & Pleasure Vessel Code 2025
Applies to commercial vessels under 24m carrying up to 12 passengers. Compliance exam, annual checks, and survey-based certification. CodedOK tracks your compliance exam schedule, equipment, crew quals, and inspection readiness.
🇮🇪 Ireland — MSO Passenger Boat Licensing
Licence classes P1–P6 based on passenger count and area. Open angling boats in smooth waters with ≤3 passengers may qualify for exemption. CodedOK tracks your licence class, survey schedule, and exemption declarations.
Voyage area
🇮🇪 Ireland exemption candidate?
Open angling boats operating in smooth waters with no more than 3 passengers may qualify for exemption from Passenger Boat Licensing. CodedOK will track your exemption declarations.
ℹ️
CodedOK helps you manage compliance evidence and deadlines. It does not certify your vessel or replace official surveys. UK coding does not automatically cover Irish operation — you must meet each jurisdiction's requirements separately.
