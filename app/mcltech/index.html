Manifesto

What we believe.

01

Traditional industries deserve great software too.

The trades, the trawlers, the surveyors. They built our world. They use the worst software in it. We change that — by being there at the harbour, the yard, the workshop, and shipping software that respects their time.

02

Made in Cushendall, not Silicon Valley.

We sit beside the people we build for. We're a boat-builder's drive from McCollam Marine's office. Software made by people who can name the boats it tracks.

03

Public pricing. Plain English. Real respect.

No demos to see the price. No buzzwords. No upsells dressed as features. If you couldn't explain it at the harbour pub, we won't ship it.
