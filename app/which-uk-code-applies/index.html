Which UK Code applies to your vessel?

Two UK Codes cover small commercial vessels under 24 m — the new SPV Code 2025 and Workboat Code Ed.3. Plus Irish PBL across the water. Four questions and we'll tell you which one is yours.

Question 1 of 4

Is the vessel under 24 metres load-line length?

The threshold is load-line length, not waterline. Check the builder's plate or stability book.

Question 2 of 4

How is the vessel chartered?

If it varies through the year, pick the broadest type that ever applies.

Question 3 of 4

What is the primary use?

The use the vessel is built around — not the occasional one.

Question 4 of 4

Where does it operate?

Operating area drives the Category (UK) or Class (Irish PBL).
