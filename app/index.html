The new code is here.
Are you ready?

For twenty years, UK coded vessels were certified under MGN 280. From December 2025, the new MCA Sport & Pleasure Vessel Code Edition 1 takes over — and every charter operation faces five new requirements.

01
Mandatory SMS for charter
An 11-section Safety Management System, required for every operation regardless of length.
02
New stability route
Single declaration route for under-15m vessels — your old declaration may not pass.
03
Refreshed equipment tables
EPIRB now Cat 0–2 (was 0–1). Life raft certificates on file. Updated first aid spec.
04
Formal owner declarations
Four signed, dated, version-controlled declarations — operating area, persons, stability, charter type.
05
Transition window
12 months for existing certs — but new vessels, change-of-owner, or change-of-use must use the new Code from day one.
⚠
The cost of falling behind
A missed renewal cancels a charter. A failed survey loses you months. The transition year is when surveyors flag the most issues.

FAQ

Honest answers.

What changes on 1 December 2025?

The MCA Sport & Pleasure Vessel Code Edition 1 replaces MGN 280. New mandatory Safety Management System for charter, new stability route for under-15m, refreshed equipment tables, formal owner declarations. Full explainer →

Does it cover Irish PBL?

Yes. All six Classes (P1–P6) are first-class jurisdictions per vessel. Pick "Ireland — PBL" and the Marine Survey Office schedule loads alongside the UK MCA set. Class breakdown →

What if my certificates are on paper?

Perfect — that's what CodedOK is for. Photograph each one. AI reads issuer, expiry, serial number, and files it against the right vessel. Works on creased, faded, or partially-obscured paperwork.

Can my surveyor see this?

Only when you invite them. Surveyor seat is free per vessel. They see your record from their own login, sign off the inspection in the app, and the signed pack lands in your inbox.

Does it work offshore with no signal?

Yes. Installed as a PWA, the app works fully offline. Edits queue locally and sync when you're back in coverage. No data lost in a tunnel or twenty miles offshore.

Is my data secure?

Evidence is stored in Supabase EU (Frankfurt), GDPR-aligned. Cached on your device for offline use. Export as PDF archive any time. Full erasure within 30 days on request.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Cancel from inside the app. Solo is free forever; Pro and Team have a 14-day trial.
