When does the iPhone app launch?

Very soon — it's being prepared for the App Store now. Founding-list members are emailed the moment it's live, and get access first.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free for one vessel, forever. Founding skippers also get 3 months of Pro (unlimited vessels and extra features) free from launch.

Who is it for?

Owners, operators and skippers of coded vessels in the UK and Ireland — charter boats, RIB tours, dive and angling boats, workboats — working under the MCA Codes of Practice.

What about Android?

CodedOK works today on any phone through your browser at app.codedok.com, and installs to your home screen like an app. A Play Store version is planned.

What will you do with my email?

Tell you when the app launches and send your founding offer. Anything more only if you ticked the box. Unsubscribe any time. See our privacy policy.
