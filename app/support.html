Common questions

How do I add a certificate?
Tap Add a record on the Home screen (or the + button), then Take a photo. CodedOK reads the certificate and fills in the type, number and expiry date for you. You can also upload a PDF, or type the details in manually.
How do renewal reminders work?
Once a record has an expiry date, CodedOK reminds you at 90, 60, 30 and 7 days before it runs out — by push notification and email. Turn notifications on from the Account screen to get them on your phone.
How do I delete my account?
Open the Account tab and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. You can also email privacy@codedok.com and we'll action it within 30 days.
How do I upgrade or manage my plan?
Solo is free forever for single-vessel skippers. To run multiple vessels or a team, manage your plan any time at codedok.com.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything you add is saved on your device first and syncs automatically when you're back in signal — so it works on the pontoon, in the yard, or out of range.
Is my data safe?
Your data is hosted in the EU and protected by row-level security, so only you (and people you invite) can see your fleet. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.
I forgot my password
On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we'll email you a reset link. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or email hello@codedok.com.
