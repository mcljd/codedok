Medium (120 words)

CodedOK is the first compliance app built around the new MCA Sport & Pleasure Vessel Code 2025 — the regulation that replaces MGN 280 for UK coded vessels from December 2025. It also covers the Workboat Code Edition 3 and the Irish Marine Survey Office Passenger Boat Licence (PBL) Classes P1–P6.

Operators photograph paper certificates and CodedOK's AI extracts the expiry, issuer and serial number — no retyping. Surveyors get free cross-org access to client vessels for sign-off inside the app. The app is offline-first, built as a Progressive Web App for the wheelhouse, with renewal reminders firing 90 / 60 / 30 days before expiry. Founded 2026, based in Cushendall, Northern Ireland.

What we built

Plain-English summary you can paraphrase or use directly.

  • AI certificate scanner — point a phone at any paper certificate; the app extracts expiry, issuer, serial number in about a second.
  • Survey importer — drop in a surveyor's PDF report; the app parses every certificate and deficiency into a structured record.
  • Multi-code coverage — one record per vessel, valid against MCA SPV 2025, Workboat Code Edition 3, and Irish PBL P1–P6 simultaneously.
  • Renewal reminders — push notifications and email at 90, 60, and 30 days before expiry.
  • Surveyor handoff — surveyors get a free seat per vessel they're invited to. They sign off inside the app and the signed inspection pack lands in the operator's inbox.
  • Offline-first — works fully offline, syncs when signal returns. Built for use at sea.
  • Safety Management System builder — AI drafts a tailored 11-section SMS from a six-question wizard, in the operator's house style.
